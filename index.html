Selected Work

Rubric Data Pipeline & AI-Assisted Annotation · Ant Group, 2025.07 – 2026.01

Built the human-in-the-loop data production and model-optimisation loop for empathetic medical dialogue: expert alignment, dynamic rubric generation, GPT-assisted query rewriting, and LLM-as-judge pairwise ranking, with rubrics reused as the reward signal for RL.

Also led a LangChain/ReAct workflow that automates medical text annotation, and a three-tier automated QC pipeline whose SFT-tuned multimodal fact-checking raised data acceptance accuracy by 50%.

Annotation accuracy +40% Throughput +50% SFT +2.24 pt RL +5.63 pt Thumbs-up/down +6.5% to +13% Full rollout

SmartMapNet · Smart Auto, 2024.06 – 2025.06

Online vectorized HD-map perception covering lane lines, parking-space lines, low-lying static obstacles and traffic signs from 7 surround-view cameras plus LiDAR, on Nvidia Orin X.

MapTR-based encoder/decoder with LSS view transformation, BEVSeg and PVSeg auxiliary supervision, VDDL loss for geometric distortion, a dedicated two-stage polygon matcher for low obstacles, and PTQ post-training quantization for deployment.

7V + LiDAR Orin X PTQ quantized Shipped on AVP / LP

Parking_OneNet · Smart Auto, 2022.09 – 2024.09

Single multi-task 2D network over four fisheye IPM bird's-eye views detecting in-space static obstacles — wheel stops, ground locks — and wall/column ground contact points, deployed on Orin N.

Customised angle prediction with KLD loss and PSC encoding, RANSAC contact-point refinement, AB-point and depth-based pose correction, corner-rotation handling for tilted scenes, and Kalman fusion to stop box jitter at IPM seams.

4× fisheye IPM Orin N >95% parking success APA / RPA / RSPA production
